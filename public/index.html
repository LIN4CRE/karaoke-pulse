🎤 How to Use KaraokePulse in 4 Easy Steps

1
🎵

Pick Any Song

Click any song from Top Hits on the left, or type any artist in Search YouTube. Songs with lyrics are marked with green badges.

2
🎙️

Turn On Your Mic

Click Mic: Off in the top header. Your voice plays live through the speakers with authentic karaoke echo! (Headphones recommended to avoid feedback).

3
✨

Adjust Vocals

Want only instruments? Leave Vocal Reducer at 0%. Want backup singers to help you? Slide to 25% for Guide Vocals.

4
🔴

Record Your Take

Click Record Take to record your voice singing over the track. When you finish, listen back and download your performance!

⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts: Spacebar = Play/Pause • Left/Right Arrows = Skip 5s • Click any lyric to jump
